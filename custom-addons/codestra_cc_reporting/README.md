# Codestra Contact Center Reporting

Versioned campaign KPI definitions and immutable, source-hashed snapshots for
operational, workforce, quality, compliance, and integration reporting.

Agent access is limited to their own agent-level snapshots. Supervisors and WFM
remain campaign-scoped. Global aggregates do not contain customer identifiers.
Raw export is disabled; controlled export requests retain reason, scope, row
count, checksum, and expiry evidence without returning business rows.
